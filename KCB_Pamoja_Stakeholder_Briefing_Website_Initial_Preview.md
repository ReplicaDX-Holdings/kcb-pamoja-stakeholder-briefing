## Identity correction

The first briefing version displayed an unapproved Pamoja construction. It used larger loop geometry and a 9px connector rather than the clean Connected Utility master selected for the product. The source builder and generated HTML have now been corrected.

All four relevant briefing applications now use the exact primary master: a KCB Navy rounded-square field, the paired green and white loops, and the centered 6px green connector. The obsolete geometry is absent from the generated HTML and from the final desktop and 390px mobile renders.

The KCB relationship is represented separately through the official KCB Group logo asset and the endorsement copy “KCB Group initiative” with “For People. For Better.” The endorsement follows the submitted digital guideline treatment and does not replace, redraw, or merge the Pamoja product mark with the corporate logo. The mobile treatment places the endorsement in a contained light panel so the corporate message remains legible without overpowering the product identity.

## Core recommendation

Approve **Connected Utility** as the KCB Pamoja brand system to take into customer and operating-model validation. Use **Regional Clarity** as the market and corridor expression and **Calm Momentum** as the progress, status, and recovery behaviour.

## Opportunity and POC frame

The briefing treats diaspora as the strongest current evidence base, intra-Africa trade as a credible but corridor-specific opportunity, and AFCON 2027 as a confirmed East African attention window rather than a sponsorship, demand forecast, or universal route claim.

The proposed 2027 portfolio comprises a corridor and compliance control tower; diaspora purpose-payment testing; regional SME payment and reconciliation testing; rights-safe AFCON local-currency spend and travel support; and cross-cutting fraud-resilience and inclusion testing.
